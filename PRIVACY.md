# Privacy Policy

*Effective November 11, 2026*

Asciiquarium is published by Make It Appen LLC. This policy covers the Asciiquarium screen saver for
macOS and the Asciiquarium app for Apple TV.

## What we collect

Nothing. Asciiquarium doesn't collect, store or send any personal information or usage data. It has
no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet
itself. (On the Mac, the Credits link opens the project's web page in your browser.)

## What stays on your device

Your Options choices (fish amount, creature set, special creatures, background and text size) are
saved on your Mac or Apple TV so they're there the next time you open it. They never leave the
device, and deleting the app or screen saver removes them.

## Children

Asciiquarium collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
